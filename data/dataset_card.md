# Dataset Card — Transaction Intelligence (synthetic descriptors)

**Version:** built from `configs/data.yaml` · seed `20260626` · `noise_level=1.0` ·
`rows_per_merchant=20`. Regenerate with `make data` (deterministic). See
[docs/01-data-pipeline.md](../docs/01-data-pipeline.md).

## Summary
Synthetic, labeled bank/card **transaction descriptor strings** for single-label multi-class
categorization (category + subtype). Generated by running a merchant **gazetteer** through a
probabilistic **noise model**, then split leakage-safely by merchant. The gazetteer is a
hand-curated seed (6 merchants/subtype) **plus an LLM-generated expansion** (the `expand-gazetteer`
workflow — 40 parallel Sonnet agents — deduped) for the merchant diversity needed to generalize.

## Composition
- **31,100 rows**, **1,555 merchants**, **11 categories / 40 subtypes** (24–40 merchants/subtype).
- Splits (by **merchant group** — no merchant spans splits):

  | split | rows | merchants |
  |-------|------|-----------|
  | train | 21,660 | 1,083 |
  | val   | 4,720  | 236 |
  | test  | 4,720  | 236 |

- Columns: `descriptor` (text, model input), `amount` (signed float; negative = debit), `is_debit`
  (bool), `category`, `subtype` (namespaced), `merchant_id` (group key — *not* a feature),
  `source` (`synthetic`), `noise_level`.

## Statistics (train)
- Descriptor length: min 2 / median 20 / max 70 chars.
- Unique descriptors: 77.6% of rows. Debit share: 87.4% (credits = `income/*`, some `transfers`).
- Category share 7.3%–12.7% (driven by #subtypes/category) → mild, realistic imbalance; headline
  metric is **macro-F1**.

## Splitting methodology
Per-subtype group assignment (~70/15/15 of each subtype's merchants → train/val/test). Guarantees
**(a)** no merchant in two splits (no memorization leakage) and **(b)** every subtype present in
every split. Enforced by `tests/test_splits.py`.

## Provenance / license
Curated seed + LLM-generated merchant names, all well-known/plausible public brands used
illustratively to ground synthetic data. **No real customer or transaction data.** All rows are
programmatically generated.

## Known limitations
- **Synthetic-to-real gap** — watch `test` vs `gold` divergence ([ADR 0004](../docs/decisions/0004-synthetic-first-data.md)).
- **LLM-generated merchants** may include less-common or B2B entities; deduped but not hand-verified
  one by one.
- **Gold overlap rose after expansion** — some merchants once "unseen" in the gold set are now in
  the gazetteer, so the **`test` split** (clean group split) is the cleaner measure of unseen-merchant
  generalization; gold measures realistic-text transfer.
- **MCC** is attached in the gazetteer but not yet emitted as a column/feature.
